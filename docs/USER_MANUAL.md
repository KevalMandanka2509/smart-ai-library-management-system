# User Manual

Welcome to the Smart AI Library Management System! This guide is for Students and Members.

## Getting Started

### 1. Login
- Navigate to the login page.
- Select the **Student/Member** role if prompted (usually routed automatically).
- Enter your registered email and password.
- If you forgot your password, use the "Forgot Password" link to receive an OTP to your email.

### 2. Browse & Search Books
- On the **Books** dashboard, you can view the entire catalogue.
- Use the **Search Bar** to look for specific titles, authors, or genres.
- Use the **Filters** to narrow down books (e.g., only "Available" books).
- Click on a book to view detailed information such as total copies and availability.

### 3. Borrowing Books
- Members cannot issue books themselves. 
- Please take the physical book to the Librarian's desk. They will scan your library card/ID and issue the book to you.
- Once issued, it will appear in your **My Books** or **Borrows** list on your dashboard.

### 4. Returning Books
- Bring the book back to the library desk. 
- The Librarian will mark it as returned.
- Ensure you return it on or before the **Due Date** to avoid fines.

### 5. Renewing Books
- If permitted by the library, you can request a renewal.
- Go to your active borrows and click **Renew**. 
- You cannot renew a book if someone else has reserved it, or if you have reached the maximum renewal limit.

### 6. Reserving Books
- If a book has 0 available copies, you can click **Reserve**.
- You will be added to the waitlist. 
- You will receive a notification when the book is returned and available for you to pick up.

### 7. View & Pay Fines
- Go to the **Fines** section from your sidebar.
- You will see a list of unpaid fines if you returned a book late.
- Click **Pay** to settle the fine (note: in this system, this simulates a payment gateway or desk payment).
- If your fines exceed the library's limit, your account may be blocked from borrowing new books.

### 8. Notifications
- Click the **Bell Icon** on the navigation bar to see your notifications (e.g., due date reminders, reservation availability).

### 9. AI Chatbot
- Click the **Chat icon** to open the AI Assistant.
- You can ask questions in English, Gujarati, or Hinglish!
- **Examples**: 
  - "What are my current fines?"
  - "Do you have any books on Python?"
  - "मारी કઈ પુસ્તકો borrow માં છે?"
- The chatbot accesses live library data and provides instant, personalized answers.
